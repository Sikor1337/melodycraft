Privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026

We keep this short because we collect little: only what we need to make and deliver your song.

What we receive

  • Your song brief — the style, the occasion, who the song is for and your story. When you check out, it is emailed to us through the form service Web3Forms. A copy also stays in your own browser, on your device.
  • Your payment is handled by Stripe. From Stripe we receive your name, your email address and whether the payment went through — never your card number.
  • Your emails to us, with your email address.

Why

To make your song, deliver it, handle revisions and answer your questions. Nothing else: no newsletters, and we never sell or share your details for advertising.

Who else handles it

Stripe (payments), Web3Forms (delivers your brief to our inbox), our email provider, and Cloudflare, which hosts this site and counts visits without cookies. Song samples and cover art load from Spotify when you view or play them.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. We don’t track you across other websites, and we let no advertising or tracking service do so here. That is also why browsers that send a “Do Not Track” signal are treated the same as all others.

Children

MelodyCraft is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to deliver your song and handle revisions, and longer only where the law requires it.

Your choices

Email hello@melodycraft.io to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. We reply within one month.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
