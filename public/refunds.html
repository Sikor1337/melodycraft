Refunds & revisions

Updated 13 November 2026

Revisions

Every plan includes 2 revisions, so we can fine-tune your song until it feels right.

Full refund

  • if you cancel before our producer starts working on your song, or
  • if we don’t deliver your song.

After delivery

Once your song is delivered, we fix it with the included revisions instead of refunding it — a song made only for you can’t be resold. Most songs are delivered within 24 hours.

How to ask

Email hello@melodycraft.io from the address you paid with. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe.
